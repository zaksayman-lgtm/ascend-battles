About Ascend Battles

Ascend Battles is an automated project matchmaking and 1v1 prediction trading platform built directly on Ascend (@AscendLaunch)—the next-generation launchpad for the Elysium ecosystem.


How It Works:

  • Web3 Wallet Sync: Connect your EVM/Elysium wallet seamlessly.
  • Free XP System: Claim your welcome airdrop of +200 XP to predict project performance with zero financial risk.
  • Predict Matchups: Back top projects launching on Ascend (like Incentiv, Mavryk, or Verse8) in head-to-head 1v1 battles.
  • Leaderboards & Rewards: Accumulate XP for accurate predictions, climb global ranks, and position yourself for future ecosystem token yield and allocations.
